TAMING AI FLASHCARD TEMPLATE

10 blank cards: 3 About AI, 3 AI Can Help, 4 Wise AI Choices.
Two extra pages are examples and do not count toward the learner deck.

In Canva, make one copy per group. Replace Short title and One short sentence. Remove the Filipino placeholder hints and the Your picture/Iyong larawan text when adding the final content. Keep the category labels. Add a learner drawing, a free Canva picture or one of the group's two AI pictures inside the four corner marks.

Keep one idea per card and use large, simple wording. A facilitator should check every claim with the group.

Printing: the A4 file has four cards per sheet, each 90 x 135 mm. Print at 100% / Actual size and trim using the marks. Pages 11 and 12 in the single-card PDF are examples.

The three backgrounds were made with the built-in image generation tool. The prompt set is included in assets/generation-prompts.json. Text in the Canva design remains editable; the decorative frame and Robi are background images.
